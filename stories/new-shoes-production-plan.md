# الحذاء الجديد — خطة إنتاج وتجربة مجانية
## Story: The New Shoes

**Format:** 9:16, Iraqi social drama, 5 scenes × 8 seconds = 40 seconds.
**Main characters:** Hassan, nine-year-old Iraqi boy, short black hair, brown eyes, faded school uniform; his mother, early 30s, simple dark headscarf and modest clothes.

### Scene 1 — الحذاء الممزق (8s)
Prompt: Photorealistic vertical 9:16 cinematic shot, early morning in an old Baghdad neighborhood. A 9-year-old Iraqi schoolboy with short black hair and a faded uniform walks to school wearing visibly torn black shoes. Tracking camera at waist height, then low close-up of the shoes. Realistic Iraqi architecture, natural footsteps, distant schoolchildren, gentle sad piano, no dialogue. Keep Hassan's appearance and wardrobe consistent.

### Scene 2 — واجهة المحل (8s)
Prompt: Same Iraqi boy Hassan stops outside an authentic small shoe shop in Baghdad, looking longingly at a new pair of black school shoes behind the glass. Close-up of his hopeful brown eyes reflected in the window. Soft street ambience, cinematic golden light. Spoken Iraqi Baghdadi Arabic, Hassan whispers exactly: «يا ريت لو إلي». No extra words, clear child voice, synchronized lips.

### Scene 3 — الهدية (8s)
Prompt: Morning at the entrance of Hassan's humble Baghdad home. He discovers a small paper bag hanging on the door handle, opens it and finds new black shoes identical to the shop display. Include a note visually only if text rendering is accurate: «من شخص يعرف شكد أنت قوي». Close-up on hands and joyful face. Birds, bag rustling, gentle piano. No dialogue.

### Scene 4 — الحقيقة (8s)
Prompt: Inside the modest Iraqi home, Hassan returns wearing his new shoes. His mother sits sewing customers' clothes, with tired hands. He spots the shoe-store receipt beside her, looks from the receipt to his mother, realizes the truth. Slow emotional push-in, stitching and room ambience. Hassan asks in authentic Iraqi Baghdadi Arabic exactly: «يمّه، إنتِ اشتريتيلي الحذاء؟» Natural child voice. No extra dialogue.

### Scene 5 — حضن الأم (8s)
Prompt: Same mother and Hassan inside the room, she smiles despite her exhaustion. She replies softly in natural Iraqi Baghdadi Arabic exactly: «إي يمّه، أريدك تمشي ورا أحلامك، مو ورا وجع رجليك». Hassan embraces her. Golden afternoon light, intimate close-up, slow pull-back, gentle strings, believable crying and emotion, exact lip sync, no extra dialogue. Add end title in the video editor, not in AI generation: «بعض الأمهات يشترن لأطفالهن أحذية… ويدفعن ثمنها من راحتهن».

Five 8-second story scenes cannot be costed at 98 points/6 seconds because this is a different duration. For any chosen model, calculate: daily clips = floor(150 / actual tokens per clip). Story days = ceil(required clips / daily clips), assuming no other token use.

## Practical workflow
1. Generate and keep one consistent character reference for Hassan and one for mother.
2. Create a still frame for each scene.
3. Animate each scene where a free or affordable model permits it.
4. For Iraqi dialogue, test one brief line first; use Google Flow or a voiceover workflow if model speech is poor.
5. Review pronunciation, facial consistency and clothing before continuing.
6. Edit 9:16 clips together, add sound design and end title, export MP4.
